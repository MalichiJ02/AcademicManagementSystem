# Five-Minute Presentation Demo Script

## Prepare before presenting

Use only administrator-created fictional test information. Start the application, create the first Administrator account at Setup, then prepare one programme, the three study modes, two or three courses, a lecturer, a room, one Coordinator account, and one Student account. Do not use real student details or real payment information.

## 1. Opening — 20 seconds

This is an Intelligent Academic Management System with Timetable Optimization prototype for selected academic workflows. It does not replace the CBU portal and does not process real payments.

## 2. Empty start and administrator setup — 45 seconds

1. Show that a new installation opens the Setup page rather than a fictional student dashboard.
2. Sign in as the Administrator.
3. Open Academic setup and point out the configurable 30% registration, 80% exam-slip, 100% results, K600 late fee, four-course total, and two-carry normal limits.
4. Show Full-time, Part-time with a maximum of two courses, and Evening with its own payment account.

## 3. Student invoice and self-registration — 70 seconds

1. Sign in with the prepared Student account.
2. Open Invoices and generate the student's own invoice.
3. Show the programme fee, study-mode fee, payment account, payment percentage, and balance.
4. Return to Administrator Finance and record a fictional payment that reaches 30%.
5. Return to the Student portal and open Registration.
6. Select an eligible course and submit the registration.
7. Explain that the student, not the coordinator, performs normal registration. The system checks payment, programme, study mode, prerequisite passes, semester offering, carry courses, course limits, and timetable conflicts.

## 4. Timetable optimization — 60 seconds

1. Sign in as the Coordinator and open Timetable optimizer.
2. Show a course awaiting allocation.
3. Generate a conflict-aware allocation.
4. Explain that the optimizer rejects invalid room, lecturer, capacity, and availability choices before ranking student conflicts.
5. Point out that an Evening-only course is assigned only to evening slots. Carry-course conflicts are still minimized, but after ordinary student conflicts.

## 5. Exam slip and results gate — 50 seconds

1. Return to the Student portal and open Exam Slip while payment remains between 30% and 80%.
2. Show the clear locked explanation.
3. As Coordinator, enter and publish a fictional result for the confirmed registration.
4. Show that the result remains financially locked for the Student until the total payment reaches 100%.

## 6. Closing — 15 seconds

The prototype demonstrates administrator-managed academic data, individual student access, student-generated invoices, financial gates, self-registration, progression checks, and conflict-aware timetable allocation. The remaining production work is integration, institutional validation, stronger timetable optimization, and load testing.
